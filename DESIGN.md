# Forma Studio design

- Role: book a fictional creative session with service, staff and available time visible in one flow.
- Voice: warm and useful. Name the choice and its result. State clearly that this public demo makes no real booking or payment.
- Type: DM Sans for controls and reading; Space Grotesk for strong display only. Keep form labels readable.
- Color: warm paper, dark ink, blue selection and coral studio artwork. Color shows active choices before it decorates.
- Layout: booking form is the main event. Service choice, calendar and confirmation read in that order on phone and desktop.
- Motion: selected slots and booking feedback only; honor reduced motion.
